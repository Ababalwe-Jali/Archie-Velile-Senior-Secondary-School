Alumni Network

A Community That Endures Beyond the Gates

The relationship between Archie Velile Senior Secondary School and its graduates does not end at matriculation—it continues to grow. Our alumni network exists to keep former learners connected to one another and to the school that shaped their early years, creating a living community that spans generations, professions, and continents.

Maintaining these lifelong relationships strengthens the school as a whole. Alumni return as mentors, donors, and role models, offering current learners a living example of where an Archie Velile education can lead, while gaining a lasting sense of belonging to something greater than themselves.

Whether you graduated last year or decades ago, you remain part of our story. We invite every former learner to stay involved—through mentorship, giving back, sharing milestones, or simply reconnecting with old classmates and educators who helped shape who you are today.

A Message to Our Alumni

Your Story Is Still Part of Ours

To every former learner of Archie Velile Senior Secondary School—wherever you are and whatever path you have taken—thank you for carrying our values out into the world. The discipline, integrity, and sense of purpose instilled here have travelled with you into universities, workplaces, families, and communities far beyond Dimbaza.

We invite you to remain close to your school. Reconnect with the classmates and educators who shaped your journey. Celebrate the milestones you have reached since leaving our gates, and consider how you might support the learners who are walking the path you once walked.

"A school's greatest legacy is not written in its history, but lived through the graduates who continue to carry its values forward."

Your continued involvement—whether through mentorship, financial support, or simply sharing your story—helps ensure that the next generation of Archie Velile learners has the same opportunities to grow, achieve, and lead that were once given to you.

Yours in continued partnership, Mr Mgoqi Principal

with Distinction

From the classrooms of Dimbaza to boardrooms, hospitals, courtrooms, and lecture halls across the country, our alumni continue to embody the excellence and integrity Archie Velile Senior Secondary School instils in every learner.

Class of 2005
Distinguished Alumnus

Dr T. Mabaso

Cardiothoracic Surgeon · Chris Hani Baragwanath Academic Hospital

Dr Mabaso matriculated from Archie Velile Senior Secondary School in 2005 before going on to study medicine. Today, he leads a team of surgeons at one of the country's largest academic hospitals and regularly returns to Dimbaza to mentor learners pursuing careers in science and medicine.

"Everything I know about discipline and hard work, I learned first at Archie Velile."
