Build Your Career at Archie Velile

Archie Velile Senior Secondary School welcomes skilled, committed professionals who share our dedication to quality public education. Every vacancy is advertised and filled through a transparent, fair, and professional recruitment process, in line with Department of Basic Education requirements.

Our Recruitment Principles

A Fair, Transparent Process — Every Time

We hold every recruitment process to the same standard of fairness and transparency that governs our classrooms. Applicants can trust that every vacancy at Archie Velile Senior Secondary School is filled on merit, in line with the principles below.

Merit-Based
Fair & Equal Opportunity

Every application is assessed fairly and consistently. Archie Velile Senior Secondary School is an equal opportunity employer and welcomes applications from all suitably qualified candidates.

Shortlisting
Only Shortlisted Applicants Contacted

Given the volume of applications received, only candidates selected for shortlisting will be contacted. If you have not heard from us within four weeks of the closing date, please consider your application unsuccessful.

Deadlines
Closing Dates Are Strictly Observed

Late, incomplete, or emailed applications submitted after the stated closing date and time will not be considered, in keeping with our commitment to a fair process for all applicants.

Compliance
Aligned With DBE & SGB Requirements

All recruitment is conducted in accordance with the requirements of the Department of Basic Education, the Eastern Cape Department of Education, and our School Governing Body.

How It Works

Our Recruitment Process

A clear, consistent process from application to appointment — the same for every candidate, every time.

01

Application Submitted

Candidates apply online or in person before the stated closing date, with all required supporting documents.

02

Screening & Shortlisting

Applications are reviewed against the minimum requirements, and a shortlist is compiled for each vacancy.

03

Interviews

Shortlisted candidates are invited to an interview with the relevant panel, which may include the SGB.

04

Appointment

The successful candidate is notified directly and appointed in line with departmental approval processes.
