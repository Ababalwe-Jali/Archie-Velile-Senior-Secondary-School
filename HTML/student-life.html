RCL President's Message

Representing Learners. Inspiring Positive Change.

It is a privilege to serve as the Representative Council of Learners (RCL) President at Archie Velile Senior Secondary School. Our role extends beyond representing the voices of learners—we strive to foster a school community built on respect, responsibility, unity, and mutual support. Every learner deserves to feel heard, valued, and empowered to contribute positively to the life of our school.

As learner leaders, we believe that leadership begins with service. By working alongside our educators, school management, and fellow learners, we aim to create an environment where everyone is encouraged to participate, grow, and pursue excellence both inside and outside the classroom. Together, we can build a school culture that celebrates achievement, embraces diversity, and prepares every learner to become a responsible citizen and future leader.

"Education is the most powerful weapon which you can use to change the world." - Nelson Rolihlahla Mandela

These words remind us that education is not only about acquiring knowledge but also about shaping character, unlocking opportunities, and empowering individuals to make a meaningful difference in their communities. As learners, we all share the responsibility of making the most of every opportunity to learn, lead, and serve with integrity.

Yours in leadership, Siganga Ovayo President – Representative Council of Learners (RCL)

Student Life

Belonging, Culture and Growth Beyond the Classroom

Student life at Archie Velile Senior Secondary School extends far beyond academic achievement. Through sport, cultural activities, leadership opportunities, community engagement, and heritage celebrations, learners are encouraged to discover their talents, celebrate their identity, and develop the confidence to make meaningful contributions to society.

We believe that a well-rounded education nurtures not only knowledgeable learners, but also respectful, resilient, and responsible young people who embrace diversity, honour their heritage, and grow into confident leaders of tomorrow.
