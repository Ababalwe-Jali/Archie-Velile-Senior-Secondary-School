History

Rooted in Community, Built on Excellence

Founded to serve the educational needs of learners from Dimbaza and the greater King William's Town community, Archie Velile Senior Secondary School was established with a vision of providing quality public education founded on discipline, opportunity, and academic excellence. Over the years, the school has become more than a place of learning—it has become a cornerstone of the community, shaping generations of young people who have gone on to make meaningful contributions in higher education, the workplace, and society.

Through the dedication of its educators, the support of parents and guardians, and the commitment of its learners, the school has earned a reputation for fostering a culture of hard work, integrity, and continuous improvement. While remaining proud of its heritage, Archie Velile Senior Secondary School has embraced innovation by enhancing teaching and learning, strengthening learner support, and adopting modern ways of engaging with its school community.

As we look to the future, we remain committed to honouring the values upon which our school was built while preparing learners for the opportunities and challenges of a rapidly changing world. Our history continues to inspire our vision—a future where every learner is empowered to achieve excellence, lead with integrity, and make a positive impact in their communities and beyond.

Educational Standards

Committed to National Educational Excellence

Archie Velile Senior Secondary School operates within South Africa's recognised public education system, delivering quality teaching and learning aligned with national academic standards. As a public secondary school, we hold ourselves accountable to the same rigorous benchmarks that govern education across the country — from curriculum delivery through to examination readiness — ensuring every learner is prepared with integrity for the National Senior Certificate and the opportunities that follow it.

National Curriculum
Department of Basic Education

Our school follows the National Curriculum Statement (NCS) prescribed by the Department of Basic Education, ensuring every subject we teach meets the country's official academic standards.

Provincial Governance
Eastern Cape Department of Education

As a public school, we operate under the governance, administration, and support of the Eastern Cape Department of Education, which oversees public schooling across the province.

Qualification Assurance
Umalusi

Our learners are prepared for the National Senior Certificate (NSC), a qualification that is quality assured and certified by Umalusi, South Africa's Council for Quality Assurance in General and Further Education and Training.

Professional Teaching Standards
South African Council for Educators (SACE)

SACE is the professional council for educators in South Africa, promoting ethical teaching practices, professional development, and high standards across the education profession. Our educators are expected to uphold the values and Code of Professional Ethics established by SACE.
