A Structured Academic Journey from Grade 8 to Grade 12

Every learner at Archie Velile Senior Secondary School follows a carefully structured academic pathway, built on the National Curriculum Statement (CAPS) and shaped around two distinct phases of growth. From the moment a learner enters Grade 8 to the day they write their final Grade 12 examination, our academic programme is designed to build knowledge, confidence, and independence in equal measure.

Our Senior Phase (Grades 8–9) lays the intellectual and personal foundations every learner needs, while our Further Education and Training band (Grades 10–12) allows learners to specialise within one of four academic faculties, preparing them for tertiary study, vocational training, or the world of work.

Two phases, one continuous journey — each stage built to prepare learners for the one that follows.

Grades 8 – 9

Senior Phase: Building Strong Academic Foundations

The Senior Phase is where every Archie Velile learner's academic story truly begins. Across all learning areas, Grade 8 and 9 learners are guided through a broad, balanced curriculum designed to build the literacy, numeracy, and scientific thinking that underpin every subject they will encounter in the years ahead.

Beyond content knowledge, this phase places strong emphasis on critical problem-solving, independent study habits, and the confidence to ask questions and engage actively in the classroom. Teachers work closely with learners during these formative years to identify individual strengths and areas for support, ensuring no learner falls behind.

By the end of Grade 9, learners are equipped with the academic grounding and self-awareness needed to make an informed, confident choice of subject specialisation as they move into Grade 10.

Grades 10 – 12

FET Band: Specialisation, Preparation, and Purpose

In the Further Education and Training band, learners specialise within one of our four academic faculties — Mathematics & Sciences, Languages, Commerce, or Humanities — pursuing subject combinations aligned with their strengths and future ambitions. This is where academic direction sharpens into academic purpose.

These three years are supported by structured assessment programmes, dedicated extra classes, and focused revision sessions ahead of major examinations. Learner mentoring and consistent academic monitoring help each learner stay on track, while targeted examination readiness programmes build the discipline and confidence Grade 12s need most.

Whether a learner is preparing for university entrance, a vocational qualification, or direct entry into the workplace, the FET band ensures they leave Archie Velile Senior Secondary School with both the academic results and the resilience to succeed beyond the classroom.

Faculty of Mathematics & Sciences

Head of Department

Ms N. Dlamini

HOD: Mathematics & Sciences

Overview

Developing analytical thinkers and problem-solvers through a strong foundation in Mathematics and the Sciences, preparing learners for higher education and careers in science, technology, engineering, health sciences, and related fields.

A Message from the Head of Department

The Mathematics & Sciences Faculty is committed to nurturing curiosity, critical thinking, and academic excellence. Through engaging classroom instruction, practical investigations, and a culture of continuous learning, we encourage learners to ask meaningful questions, embrace challenges, and develop the confidence to solve real-world problems.

"Great discoveries begin with curious minds and disciplined thinking. Every lesson is an opportunity to unlock a learner's potential."

Whether a learner aspires to become an engineer, doctor, scientist, educator, or innovator, our faculty provides the knowledge, guidance, and academic support needed to build a strong foundation for lifelong success. We are proud to inspire a generation that thinks critically, acts responsibly, and embraces the limitless possibilities of education.

Academic Subjects

  • MathematicsDevelop analytical thinking, logical reasoning, and advanced problem-solving through mathematical concepts and applications.
  • Physical SciencesExplore the principles of Physics and Chemistry to understand the natural world through scientific investigation.
  • Life SciencesStudy living organisms, ecosystems, genetics, biodiversity, and the processes that sustain life on Earth.
  • Mathematical LiteracyApply mathematics to everyday situations involving finance, data interpretation, measurement, and informed decision-making.

HOD: Languages

Overview

Building confident communicators through the study of languages that strengthen literacy, critical thinking, creativity, and meaningful engagement with diverse communities.

A Message from the Head of Department

Language is the foundation of learning, communication, and personal expression. Within our faculty, learners develop the ability to read critically, write effectively, speak confidently, and appreciate the richness of South Africa's multilingual heritage. Every lesson equips learners with skills that extend far beyond the classroom.
