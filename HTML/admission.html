Admission Requirements

What We Look For

Admission requirements differ slightly for learners entering Grade 8 and those entering Grade 10, since each intake marks a different stage of the secondary school journey. Both intakes are considered fairly, in line with the admission policy of the Eastern Cape Department of Basic Education.

Grade 8 Applicants

For learners moving from primary to secondary school. Applicants must meet the age requirements for Grade 8 entry set out by the Department of Basic Education and provide evidence of successful completion of Grade 7.

  • Completed Grade 7, with a final school report
  • Meets the Department's age requirement for Grade 8 entry
  • Resides within, or has a documented connection to, our feeder area
  • Up-to-date immunisation record

Grade 10 Applicants

For learners completing the General Education and Training band and progressing into the Further Education and Training phase. Subject choices at this stage are guided by each learner's Grade 9 results and career interests.

  • Completed Grade 9, with a final school report
  • Meets the subject prerequisites for the desired subject stream
  • Transfer learners: most recent report and a transfer or deregistration letter
  • A short subject-guidance discussion with our Academic Office, on request

Admissions Checklist

Required Supporting Documents

Preparing the required supporting documents before beginning an application helps ensure the admissions process is completed accurately and without unnecessary delays. Please review the checklist below carefully and gather every applicable document ahead of time.

Learner Documents

Documents relating to the applying learner

Unabridged Birth Certificate

Required

A certified copy confirming the learner's identity and date of birth, used to verify eligibility for the relevant grade intake.

Most Recent School Report

Required

The learner's final school report, showing completed grade level and academic performance to date.

Transfer Letter

If applicable

Required for transfer learners moving from another school, confirming deregistration from their previous institution.

Parent / Guardian Documents

Documents confirming the parent or legal guardian

Parent or Guardian ID

Required

A certified copy of the parent or legal guardian's identity document, confirming their relationship to the learner.

Proof of Residential Address

Required

A recent utility bill, lease agreement, or affidavit confirming the family's residential address within our feeder area.

Contact Details

Required

Up-to-date telephone number and email address for the parent or guardian, used for all admissions correspondence.

Additional Supporting Documents

Required only where applicable to the learner's circumstances

Immunisation Record

If required

An up-to-date immunisation record, required for Grade 8 applicants in line with Department guidelines.

Court Documents

Where applicable

Custody, guardianship, or care order documentation, where a party other than a biological parent is applying on the learner's behalf.

Medical Information

If applicable

Relevant medical information the school should be aware of to support the learner's health and safety at school.

Disability or Learning Support Documentation

If applicable

Supporting documentation for any learning support needs, so our Academic Office can plan appropriate assistance in advance.

Before You Submit
  • All uploaded or submitted documents should be clear and legible.
  • Copies must accurately reflect the original documents.
  • Additional supporting documents may be requested where necessary.
  • Incomplete applications may delay the admissions process.
