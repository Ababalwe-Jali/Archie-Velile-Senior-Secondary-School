Academic Programmes

Four Faculties, One Standard of Excellence

At Archie Velile Senior Secondary School, academic excellence is not an aspiration — it is a daily discipline. Our curriculum is structured around four dedicated faculties, each led by an experienced Head of Department and held to a shared, measurable standard of results across every grade and every subject.

Every lesson is taught with a clear line of sight to the National Senior Certificate, university admission requirements, and the demands of the working world beyond matric. From the moment learners enter Grade 8 to the day they write their final examination, our academic programme is designed to build knowledge, confidence, and independence in equal measure.

Mathematics & Sciences · Languages

Analytical Thinking Meets Clear Communication

Our Faculty of Mathematics & Sciences — spanning Physical Sciences, Life Sciences, Mathematics and Mathematical Literacy — is built to develop learners who reason with precision. Through practical experiment, worked examples, and rigorous assessment, learners are taught not simply to memorise formulae, but to think analytically and solve problems with confidence.

Alongside this, our Faculty of Languages — offering isiXhosa, English and Afrikaans — builds the literacy and communication skills that underpin every other subject. A learner who reads, writes, and argues with clarity carries that advantage into every classroom, examination, and opportunity that follows.

Commerce

Preparing Tomorrow's Entrepreneurs and Economists

Our Faculty of Commerce — Accounting, Business Studies and Economics — introduces learners to the financial and entrepreneurial thinking that shapes the modern world. Learners develop a practical, numerate understanding of how businesses operate, how economies function, and how individuals manage money responsibly.

Case studies drawn from real South African businesses, combined with a strong grounding in ethical practice and sound decision-making, prepare learners equally well for tertiary study in accounting or economics, or for launching a business of their own straight after school.

Humanities

Building Informed, Responsible Citizens

Our Faculty of Humanities — History, Geography and Life Orientation — asks learners to look outward: at their country's past, their environment, and their responsibilities as citizens. These subjects cultivate the kind of critical thinking that questions assumptions and weighs evidence carefully, skills that matter well beyond any examination room.

Life Orientation, in particular, supports learners through the personal, social and academic pressures of adolescence, helping to develop leadership qualities, an appreciation of diversity, and the civic responsibility our communities depend on.

Admissions

Two Trusted Ways to Begin the Journey

Archie Velile Senior Secondary School offers families a choice in how they apply. Whichever route you take, every application receives the same careful review by our admissions office — there is no advantage to either method beyond your own convenience.
